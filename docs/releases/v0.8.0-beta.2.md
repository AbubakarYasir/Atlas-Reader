# Atlas Reader 0.8.0-beta.2 — Reader Workspace

This C1 Windows beta candidate turns the reader and historical workspace
prototypes into one persistent document workspace. It is intentionally held
for owner acceptance before its tag and GitHub prerelease are published.

## Highlights

- Open several PDFs in tabs and switch without discarding each reader's page,
  zoom, navigation panel, or active writing session.
- Restore open documents and their last recorded pages from the local database.
- Browse rendered page previews, PDF outline entries, local bookmarks, and
  indexed annotations from one collapsible, searchable panel.
- Jump to exact pages from every panel, the existing page field, or scrub bar.
- Choose Fit page, Fit width, or 100%, 125%, 150%, 200%, 300%, and 400% zoom in
  both reading and Write modes.
- Use a persistent sidebar on wide windows and a full-width modal navigation
  sheet on compact windows.
- Use localized English and Arabic controls with RTL layout, semantics, compact
  actions, and verified 200% text scaling.

## Owner acceptance

Open three PDFs; switch, close, and restore tabs; navigate and search every
panel; exercise every zoom mode and pan while writing; resize between wide and
compact layouts; then repeat at 200% Windows text scale with mouse and
keyboard.

Reply with an explicit PASS to authorize the `v0.8.0-beta.2` tag and GitHub
prerelease. A failure keeps C1 in progress until the defect and affected gates
are rerun.

## Important limitations

- This beta modifies local PDFs. Use copies for writing tests and keep an
  independent backup of irreplaceable documents.
- EPUB remains discovery-only.
- The two personal-file audits require owner-supplied copied Arabic and English
  PDF fixtures and are not counted as passing evidence.
- Pen, touch, HDD, and physical-printer qualification remain required at C9.
- The beta bundle is unsigned, so Windows SmartScreen may show a warning.
